## Limits of demonstration evidence

- The app is a **business-facing mobile web prototype**, not a native Android or iOS application.
- Sample data, emissions factors, financial cases and modeled benefits are **synthetic or assumptions**.
- Browser-local records do not constitute monitored operational measurements or audit-grade evidence.
- Future integrations, permissions, optimization actions, service delivery and commercial arrangements are conceptual only.

## Suggested presentation walkthrough

1. Identify the enterprise carbon visibility challenge and entrepreneurial opportunity.
2. Open **Carbon Footprint Tracker** and show a 12-month sample trend and target.
3. Add or edit an illustrative monthly observation to demonstrate tracking.
4. Show **Quick Simulator** and **Carbon Intelligence** scenario comparisons.
5. Generate a mock **GreenOps** recommendation and explain the approval guardrail.
6. Explain **Venture & Impact**: customers, value, business model, SDGs, governance, risks and scaling.
7. Conclude with limitations, evidence requirements and the proposed pilot roadmap.
